Soteriology is the study of God's work of salvation and its application to His people.

## Definition of Terms
Discussions around Salvation very frequently devolve into disagreements because people don't define their terms. Explaining what we mean by salvation can help avoid a multiplicity of headaches.

Part of the difficulty in defining Salvation is that it's both an occurrence at a particular moment, a process, and an eventual state. We can speak about, "I have been saved" (Salvation Inaugurated), "I am being saved" (Salvation Being Worked Out) and "I will be saved." (Salvation Consummated)

#### Salvation Inaugurated
> For by grace you have been saved through faith; and this _is_ not of yourselves, _it is_ the gift of God; - Ephesians 2:8

- Calling
- Regeneration
- Conversion
- Union with Christ
- Justification
- Adoption as sons of God
#### Salvation Being Worked Out
- Sanctification
- Life in the Spirit
- Perseverance
- Assurance
#### Salvation Consummated
- Resurrection
- Final judgment and vindication
- Glorification
- New creation

Generally when **we** talk about Salvation we are referring to Initial Salvation.
