Soteriology is the study of God's work of salvation and its application to His people.

## Definition of Terms
Discussions around Salvation very frequently devolve into disagreements because people don't define their terms. Explaining what we mean by salvation can help avoid a multiplicity of headaches.

Part of the difficulty in defining Salvation is that it's both an occurrence at a particular moment, a process, and an eventual state. We can speak about, "I have been saved" (Salvation Inaugurated), "I am being saved" (Salvation Being Worked Out) and "I will be saved." (Salvation Consummated)

#### Salvation Inaugurated

> For by grace you **have been saved** through faith; and this _is_ not of yourselves, _it is_ the gift of God; - Ephesians 2:8

- Calling
- Regeneration
- Conversion
- Union with Christ
- Justification
- Adoption as sons of God

#### Salvation Being Worked Out

> … but to us who **are being saved** it is the power of God. - 1 Corinthians 1:18

- Sanctification
- Life in the Spirit
- Perseverance
- Assurance

#### Salvation Consummated

> Much more then, having now been justified by His blood, we **shall be saved** from the wrath of God through Him. - Romans 5:9

- Resurrection
- Final judgment and vindication
- Glorification
- New creation

Generally when **we** talk about Salvation we are referring to Initial Salvation. 

### Justification

Justification is God's act of declaring sinners righteous on account of Christ. Through faith in Christ, we receive this right standing and have peace with God.

> Therefore, having been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ, - Romans 5:1

### Regeneration

Regeneration is the new birth. The Holy Spirit gives us new spiritual life so that we can respond to God in faith and begin to live for Him.

> He saved us … according to His mercy, by the washing of regeneration and renewing by the Holy Spirit, - Titus 3:5

### Sanctification

Sanctification is God's work of making us holy. The Holy Spirit increasingly shapes our desires and conduct to reflect Christ as we turn from sin and learn to obey Him.

> For this is the will of God, your sanctification; that is, that you abstain from sexual immorality; - 1 Thessalonians 4:3

### Glorification

Glorification is the completion of God's saving work in us. When Christ returns, we will share in His glory, free from sin and death, with bodies made like His resurrected body.

> When Christ, who is our life, is revealed, then you also will be revealed with Him in glory. - Colossians 3:4
